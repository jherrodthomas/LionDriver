# Harness box circuit simulation (ngspice pilot)

A narrow ngspice pilot on comma's harness box v3: **passive-network faults only**. The results are predictions that
sharpen the pass criteria of the bench tests VS-HW-22…24 in [WP-H-06](../WP-H-06-hardware-integration-verification.md)
§3.1. **They are not measurements and earn no FMEDA credit.** The lead asked for this scope (via the simulation session,
2026-10-27).

The predictions are in [`predictions.md`](predictions.md) (table: [`predictions.tsv`](predictions.tsv)); the nominal
netlist is [`box-v3-nominal.cir`](box-v3-nominal.cir). All three are generated by [`box_model.py`](box_model.py).

**Related model.** The simulation session's [`assurance/sim/harness_box/`](../../sim/harness_box/README.md) models the
same box from the same drawing for the DFMEA causes: SBU1 drive against an input-threshold band, R2/R3 open, Q6 short
and a D2 leak to GND, with a Falstad view. It came in on the same day as this pilot. Where the two overlap (R2/R3 open,
D2 to GND), they agree: a floating IN can settle on either side of the threshold, and a short to GND loses dominant
bits. This pilot adds the cases that model does not have, each tied to a bench step: D3 on the camera bus and its effect
on the joined stock bus, clamp shorts to VIN, the camera-bus terminations with a transient, on-resistance and ground
offset, and the bench's own SBU1 drive. Merging the two into one netlist is proposed to that session.

## 1. What the pilot says

| Finding | Basis | For |
|---|---|---|
| **The bench drive of WP-H-06 §3.1 cannot switch an intact box.** A 0–5 V source through 1 kΩ into SBU1 meets R2/R3 (240 Ω to GND), so IN reaches only 5 × 240 / 1240 ≈ 0.97 V. VS-HW-23 step 1 would see no intercept on a good box, and VS-HW-24 (b) cannot sweep IN above 1 V. A stiff source (about 10 Ω, current-limited) reaches 4.8 V and must supply about 20 mA | Ohm's law; the simulation only confirms it | WP-H-06 §3.1 set-up, bench checklist |
| The real SBU1 driver in the device must hold about 5 V into 240 Ω, so about 20 mA, to intercept | Same | WP-H-02 UK-06 |
| With R2/R3 open and SBU1 undriven, IN goes to whichever rail its leakage points at: about VIN with leakage into the pin, about 0 V with leakage out. The switch state is undefined, as RR-2026-010 F-03 says; the sign of the leakage is not known | Generic CMOS input clamps, ±1 µA | VS-HW-23 steps 2–4 |
| A camera-side clamp short (D3) on CAN2_H to GND kills dominant bits **on the joined stock bus** in pass-through, not only in intercept | DC levels | VS-HW-24 (d), DFM-021 |
| A clamp short to VIN puts a line at about 13 V, outside the −2…+7 V receiver common-mode range of ISO 11898-2, in both switch states | DC levels | VS-HW-24 (d) |
| Clamp shorts of CAN_L to GND, and 1 kΩ leakage to GND, leave readable levels | DC levels | VS-HW-24 (d) |
| Losing one camera-side termination (loopback P13–P15 or R7) still leaves 120 Ω and readable bits. Losing both leaves the camera bus unterminated in intercept: the recessive edge takes about 10 µs, past the sample point of a 500 kbit/s bit | Transient with stated bus capacitance | VS-HW-24 (c) |
| Pass-through tolerates an on-resistance of up to about 70 Ω per channel before the dominant level at the car falls under 0.9 V, with the stated transceiver model | R_on sweep | VS-HW-24 (c); DG419LE R_on still to confirm (DFM-ACT-015) |

The first two findings go to the WP-H-06 owner as a proposed change to the bench set-up; the FMEDA classification
proposals (D2/D3 short modes) go to the WP-H-03 owner. Neither document is changed here.

## 2. What the model is, and is not

| Part | Model | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| U1/U2 DG419LE | **Idealised.** Closed channel = `R_ON`, open = 1 GΩ; the case sets the state | No supply dependence, no input threshold, no behaviour outside V−…V+, no latch-up. These questions wait for the bench (VS-HW-22, VS-HW-24 a/b) and a licensable DG419LE model |
| D2/D3 BAS40SDW | Generic small-signal Schottky | Not the vendor model; forward drop and leakage approximate |
| Transceivers | Thevenin: dominant 4.5 V / 0.5 V through 25 Ω, recessive 2.5 V through 25 kΩ | Generic, inside ISO 11898-2 levels; no slope control, no fault detection |
| Car side of the camera bus | 60 Ω lumped | Assumed from the Toyota A loopback; a variant with 120 Ω inside the camera is included |
| Harness | Ideal wires | No line impedance; reflections not modelled |
| Bus capacitance | 200 pF per line per segment | Stated estimate, drives only the edge-time cases |

Every parameter and its meaning is listed at the end of [`predictions.md`](predictions.md). The netlist is
transcribed by hand from comma's published v3 drawing ([RR-2026-010](../reviews/RR-2026-010.md) §2), the same source
as the [wiring drawings](../wiring/README.md). Each box node cites the wire-end ID it stands for in
[`../wiring/wire-ends.tsv`](../wiring/wire-ends.tsv), or the wiring README §3 row for nodes those drawings do not show.
`test_box_model.py` checks the citations and that the switch pins match the wiring drawing.

## 3. Running

The pilot uses the ngspice already pinned for the supply study: ngspice 41 from the explicit conda-forge lock
[`assurance/sim/supply/supply-linux-64.lock`](../../sim/supply/supply-linux-64.lock) (every package by URL and md5),
so the repository has one ngspice, not two.

```sh
micromamba create -p ./supply-env --file assurance/sim/supply/supply-linux-64.lock
./supply-env/bin/python assurance/04-hardware/spice/box_model.py           # rebuild predictions and netlist
./supply-env/bin/python assurance/04-hardware/spice/box_model.py --check   # what CI runs
./supply-env/bin/python assurance/04-hardware/spice/test_box_model.py
```

ngspice runs in batch mode (`ngspice -b`) under subprocess. PySpice was not used: its last release (1.5, 2021) needs
the ngspice shared library and adds nothing a batch run lacks. Licences: ngspice BSD-3-Clause, PyYAML MIT (both in the
lock). The tool entry is TL-58 in [WP-P-07](../../07-supporting/WP-P-07-tool-classification-qualification.md), which now
names this pilot too.
