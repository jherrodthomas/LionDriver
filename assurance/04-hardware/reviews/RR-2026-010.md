## 1. Main result

**The harness "relay" is not a relay.** The v3 harness box ([`Harness_Box.pdf`](https://github.com/commaai/hardware/blob/master/harness/v3/Harness_Box.pdf), "car harness v3", 20 Aug 2024) uses two Vishay **DG419LEDY** CMOS analog SPDT switches, labelled *SOLID-STATE "RELAY"*. They are powered from the box supply (VIN) and controlled by the OBD-C line SBU1.

Our hardware work products, DFMEA and FMEDA assume an electromechanical intercept relay: contacts, coil, welding, contact life and drop-out bounce. That basis is wrong for this harness box.

What the drawing settles:
- **De-energised state is pass-through.** SBU1 is pulled to GND through R3 + R2 (2 × 120 Ω). When the device does not drive SBU1 (unpowered, in reset, cable unplugged), the switches sit on NC, and the camera's CAN connects to the car's CAN. This answers the harness half of UK-03 and HWSR-502a for loss of the *device*.
- **The stock camera-to-car path runs through active ICs that need power.** In pass-through, CAN_H passes U1 and CAN_L passes U2. Both switches are powered only from VIN, which for the Toyota harness comes from the ignition line through D1 (no 12 V wire in the Toyota A pinout), or from comma power. Each bus also has a Schottky clamp pair (D2, D3) to VIN and GND. **HWSR-702 ("no active component in the stock path") cannot be met by this harness box.**
- **There is no state feedback.** No line reports the switch position back to the device. SBU1 also carries the orientation-detect load, so it reads back the drive, not the switch. Readback (GAP-12, HWSR-506a, DC-03) needs a hardware change.

`commaai/neo` is the deprecated NEO research platform: a OnePlus 3 phone and an STM32F205 board, plus giraffe boards. None of it is part of the item. The `comma_3X/` folder holds only a README and mount STLs. **No comma 3X or panda (tres) schematic and no BOM is published**, so UK-01 and UK-02 stay open.

## 5. Decision for the lead

**The stock PCS/AEB path runs through powered electronics in comma's harness box. Accept that and test it, or build a LionDriver harness adapter with a passive fail-safe relay?** This is a hardware and risk-acceptance choice, so it is entered in the [decision register](../../01-management/decisions/register.yaml) for the lead.
