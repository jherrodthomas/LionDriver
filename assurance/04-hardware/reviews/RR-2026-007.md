## What changed

- **WP-W-02 v0.2:**
  - SWSR-303h and SWSR-516 now follow TSR-303 and TSR-516 as amended in WP-S-02 v0.3.
  - New SWSR-516b (failed-cancel flag).
  - New host requirements SWSR-621 to SWSR-627, including SWSR-622a.
  - New envelope requirements SWSR-711 and SWSR-711a (forwarding supervision).
  - The document now has 134 SWSRs.
- **WP-H-01 v0.2:**
  - HWSR-711 and HWSR-711a (camera-side FDCAN state observable; relay drop-out ≤ 20 ms).
  - HWSR-516 to HWSR-516b (siren path independent of the SoC, audible, testable). These close WP-H-01 OI-5.
  - HWSR-517 (own frames receivable from the bus) and HWSR-519 (NVIC priorities, cycle counter).
  - The document now has 45 HWSRs.
- **WP-C-01 v0.2:** AOU-12 and AOU-13 moved into the item definition. Until now they were defined only in WP-C-04 §11 and could not be traced.
- **Trace:**
  - `sync_requirements.py` now also generates `swsr.yaml` and `hwsr.yaml`.
  - `check_trace.py` checks the last level of K4: every TSR allocated to software has an SWSR, and every TSR allocated to hardware has an HWSR, unless the work product lists it as not refined.
  - A negative test (removing the SWSRs of TSR-711 and HWSR-517) gives the two expected K4 errors.

On the first run the new trace checks found three gaps:
- HWSR-702 named an AoU (AOU-13) that the trace data did not have;
- TSR-517 had no HWSR;
- TSR-519 had no HWSR.

All three are fixed in this change.
