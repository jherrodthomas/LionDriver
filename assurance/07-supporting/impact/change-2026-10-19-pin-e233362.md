## 1. Change

| Commit | Files | Behaviour | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| opendbc `f4e6166` | `opendbc/car/toyota/carcontroller.py` | (1) A new limit `ACCEL_FINAL_RATE_LIMIT` of 4.5 m/s³ (135 raw per `0x343` frame) applies to the final ACCEL_CMD while long control is active. The existing 4 m/s³ limits act before the PID and pitch compensation, so the command on the bus was not bounded by them. (2) While `carState.stockAeb` is set, the command is capped at 0 m/s² from the first frame, after the limit, PID and pitch compensation; braking requests pass | `opendbc/car/toyota/tests/test_carcontroller.py`, 6 cases on the reference Corolla TSS2 |
| this PR | `opendbc_repo`, WP-W-02, WP-M-11, WP-W-06, this record, the DQ-300 change record, coverage baseline, scope plots, sim results, generated trace files | Pin; SWSR-713h; OI-15 closed; OI-16 added | — |
