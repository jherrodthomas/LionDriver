## 1. Method and scope

Inventory of all 88 registered work products, including the process and trace README work products; targeted substantive review of 21 key documents. Explicit ISO references were extracted across the 185 assurance Markdown files at the assessed revision. The package holds 51 reference buckets, 368 associations, 22 documentary findings and 577 author OI rows, including rows the authors report as closed.

The source of ISO 26262:2018 was not accessible. No verified normative requirement list exists in this package. All normative verdicts remain unassessed. The record does not assert an every-clause audit was completed.

The user-supplied compliance-checker-algo was inspected at `80547e3e544eb3727489093f752f95875c8ea091`. A synthetic smoke run and four diagnostic probes demonstrated misleading coverage/method scores and compliance labels. The engine is not an authority for normative judgments.

## 2. Findings and disposition

The controlled [findings ledger](../../00-assessment/iso26262-2018/findings.csv) and [report](../../00-assessment/iso26262-2018/Audit_report.md) contain A01–A22 with source lines, reasoning, proposed responsible roles, target gates and closure evidence. Priorities P0/P1/P2 are triage categories, not automatically ISO nonconformity severities. No finding was accepted or closed by this review.

Principal issues are inaccessible normative source material; staffing and independence prerequisites; configuration and OEM assumptions that affect ASIL; missing part-specific hardware metric evidence; documented timing discrepancies; incomplete FFI and controlled Part 6 entry; HIL deferral; and absent accepted safety-case evidence.
