## File naming

`G<n>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`, one file per gate review. A repeated review of the same gate gets a new file.

## Pre-filled skeletons

[`skeletons/`](skeletons/) holds one skeleton per gate (`G0-skeleton.md` … `G6-skeleton.md`). They are generated by the [process model](../../process/README.md) (`python3 assurance/process/tools/process_model.py`) and checked by CI, so they are never edited by hand. Each skeleton follows the template below and pre-fills what the source documents already record:

- the gate's work products with their WP-M-00 status and open-item counts;
- the confirmation measures WP-M-06 assigns to the gate and whether a record exists in [`../confirmation/`](../confirmation/README.md);
- the cross-domain interfaces due by the gate, as IFR items with their owners, open item and review record (an interface is agreed only through a recorded review, DQ-129 A);
- the WP-M-02 roles still unassigned;
- the exit criteria of WP-M-00 §2, split into rows.

A skeleton is not a gate review record. To hold a review, copy the gate's skeleton to `G<n>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md` in this folder, re-check every pre-filled value at the review baseline, and complete the rest by hand.
