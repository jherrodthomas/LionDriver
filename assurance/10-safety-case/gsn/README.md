Notation: goal = rectangle; strategy = parallelogram; solution = circle (green available, amber partial, white dashed missing); context = rounded box; assumption / justification = oval (A / J); diamond = undeveloped goal. Solid arrows are SupportedBy, hollow arrows InContextOf. The colour of a goal (stripe and tint) is the worst evidence status of all solutions below it, on every sheet (green all available, amber partial, red missing, grey not developed). Defeaters (open counter-evidence, WP-K-01 §7, WP-K-03 §6) are listed in red on the goal they undermine. In the cybersecurity case the requirements that refine a goal (CSR-nnn) are shown as a context.
