# Curves on a wet road: the grip a curve at the ODD's bound needs

> **Synthetic data (DSR-12). Design aid, no evidence credit (DQ-413).** Generated by [`weather/wet_curves.py`](../weather/wet_curves.py) from the wet-braking study's friction table ([`wet-braking.md`](wet-braking.md)) and two measured tread-depth tests; do not edit by hand. Typical tyres, not this car's.

At the ODD's curve bound the car corners at 2.5 m/s² (WP-C-02 §3.2). The tyres need, on a flat curve, a friction of **0.26** to corner, **0.36** to brake at the item's 3.5 m/s² on a straight, and **0.44** to do both at once (on a 6 % bank: 0.19 and 0.40). Water on the road in the ODD's light rain: 0.20 mm.

The ODD's 3 mm of tread is a band between two measured tests: the wet stop at 3 mm is 7 % (Continental) to 35 % (MIRA) longer than with new tyres, so the grip is the new tyre's divided by 1.07 to 1.35.

| Tyres | Wet friction at 60 / 70 / 80 / 90 / 100 / 110 km/h |
|---|---|
| New tyres (8 mm) | 0.64 / 0.62 / 0.61 / 0.60 / 0.59 / 0.57 |
| 3 mm, Continental's test (+7 % stop) | 0.60 / 0.58 / 0.57 / 0.56 / 0.55 / 0.54 |
| 3 mm, MIRA's tests (+35 % stop) | 0.47 / 0.46 / 0.45 / 0.44 / 0.43 / 0.43 |
| 1.6 mm, legal minimum (outside the ODD) | 0.42 / 0.35 / 0.28 / 0.20 / 0.20 / 0.20 |

Braking the 3 mm tyres still allow while holding the curve at the bound, wet, at 110 km/h: 3.4 (MIRA) to 4.6 m/s² (Continental).

## What this shows (generated)

- **Cornering alone holds** with 3 mm of tread at every ODD speed: at least 0.43 against 0.26 needed.
- **Braking at the item's rate on a straight holds** at every ODD speed, even at MIRA's end (0.43 against 0.36).
- **Cornering at the bound and braking at the item's rate together** need 0.44. Continental's end holds at every speed; MIRA's falls just short at speed (0.43), leaving 3.4 m/s² of braking at 110 km/h instead of 3.5. The stability control would step in before the car ran wide.
- New tyres hold every case (at least 0.57); at the 1.6 mm legal minimum even cornering alone falls short at speed (0.20), which is why the ODD asks for 3 mm.
- For WP-C-02 §3.2: the 0.5 m/s² curve margin was set for controller error and road crown, not for braking in the curve on a wet road. The case that does not fit is a narrow one (a hard brake at the curve bound, in rain, on tyres at the ODD's minimum); it is a note for the curve bound's rationale rather than a reason to move it, unless measured data for this car's tyres says otherwise.
