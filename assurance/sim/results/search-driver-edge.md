# Search: Driver edge cases: wrong-direction first correction, over-correction, braking instead of steering

> **Synthetic data (DSR-12).** Generated by [`search/search.py`](../search/search.py) from [`search/families.yaml`](../search/families.yaml); do not edit by hand. Fault-injection plant model with the real panda safety code; EPS, driver and vehicle are models with stated assumptions ([plant results](plant-results.md), Model inputs). **No evidence credit.** The share of departing samples is a property of the uniform box below, not a probability of anything on the road.

The driver-model options of plant/simulate.py (DRIVER_OPTIONS, off in every catalog case) applied to three faults in a curve inside the ODD. `k` sets the intensity of the chosen behaviour: a wrong-direction first correction of 0.1 to 0.5 s; over-correction as a return-to-centre loop damped at 0.6 down to 0.15 (critically damped is 1); braking at -3 m/s² instead of steering. The run lasts 10 s after the fault so an over-correction shows its overshoot.

Faults: F-01b hard-over (right), F-04b SoC stall, F-07 EPS fault. Robustness: rho = (lane width − 1.781 m) / 2 − max |lateral offset| after the fault; rho < 0 means the body edge crossed a lane line. Beyond about −2 m the car is in the next lane and the magnitude means little; the sign and the time to the line are what count.

What the plant cannot show: it has **no tyre friction limit** (a linear tyre: a wet or icy road, a braking driver in a curve and a correction of 4 m/s² all behave as on a dry road, so nothing here says whether the car would slide) and **no perception** (the lane-keeping controller sees its lane exactly, so no perception insufficiency, wrong line or late detection is searched; the faults are injected at the actuator side). The driver is a model with stated reactions, the EPS response to each fault is assumed (M-01, M-02), and road users beside the ego are not modelled: the time to the lane line is how soon an adjacent vehicle or barrier would be reached, not whether it is hit.
