What the model detects at a given size is learned, not computed: this is the size it has to work with.

## What this shows (generated)

- **For the item to stop on its own** at −3.5 m/s², the model has to take the stopped car as a lead by 149 m, where it is **11 px wide** in the narrow frame: well under the 32 × 32 px below which MS COCO counts an object as small, the size range where detectors do worst.
- **For the driver**, an unwarned 2.5 s plus full braking needs 138 m dry and 188 m wet with 3 mm of tread; the car is 9 px wide at the wet distance. The driver's own eyes do the work there; the model's output does not.
- The car reaches 32 px in the narrow frame only at 51 m, 1.7 s away: closer than every stopping distance above. For the item to stop, the model has to act on a car smaller than that; a driver's eyes resolve far more.
