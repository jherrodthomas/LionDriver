The ego closes on a lead `dv_kph` slower, ahead at the time gap `gap_s` at t = 0, when a fault soft disable or the DM stop starts at `t_fault` (alert and slow-down as in handover-follower). The soft disable hands control back after 3 s (4 s in DQ-407 C) whatever the ACC was doing, so the braking for the lead ends there; the DM stop keeps braking to a stop. A driver who ignores the alert (believing ACC still active, HE-07.3) is cued only by the missing deceleration, the siren and the closing (TTC 2 s); one who takes over brakes after the reaction time.

Faults: ='dm_stop' if timing == 'dm-stop' else 'soft_disable'. Robustness: rho = the smallest bumper gap to the lead after the fault; rho < 0 means contact. Past contact the plant lets the vehicles overlap, so a negative magnitude means little; the sign, the time to contact and the closing speed at contact are what count.

What the plant cannot show: it has **no tyre friction limit** (the driver's braking of up to 8 m/s² and the lead's are taken as given on any road; on a wet road (W02) about 5 m/s² is what the tyres give, so a row that needs more is worse there) and **no perception** (the ACC sees its lead at once, exactly and at any range, so a lead detected late, a stopped vehicle not taken as a lead (SH-06) or a cut-in is not searched; low visibility acts only through the driver). The ACC is a model, not openpilot's MPC; the lead brakes at a constant rate; the following driver brakes after a reaction time to the ego's brake lights (on above 1 m/s²) at a set deceleration until no faster than the ego, and does not steer round it. The driver is a model with stated cues and reactions; the PCM response is assumed (M-03).
