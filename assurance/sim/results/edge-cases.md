# Edge-case catalogue: lateral departures

> **Synthetic data (DSR-12).** Generated by [`search/search.py`](../search/search.py) from [`search/families.yaml`](../search/families.yaml); do not edit by hand. Fault-injection plant model with the real panda safety code; EPS, driver and vehicle are models with stated assumptions ([plant results](plant-results.md), Model inputs). **No evidence credit.** A row is the worst case a seeded search found in its box, not a likely event: nothing here is an exposure or a probability.

The worst cases of every search family of [`families.yaml`](../search/families.yaml) (WP-V-04 U-4), each re-run with its parameters rounded as a scene writes them, without its fault (is it the fault or the road?), and under assumption probes (which stated assumption decides the outcome). Each row is mapped to the HARA hazardous event (HE-, [WP-C-03](../../02-concept/WP-C-03-hara.md)) or SOTIF hazard (SH-, [WP-C-05](../../02-concept/WP-C-05-sotif-hazard-identification.md)) it falls under. A departure that matches no hazardous event is a finding for the HARA: it is listed under [New-hazard findings](#new-hazard-findings) and queued in the [decision register](../../01-management/decisions/register.yaml); the HARA itself is not edited here.

What the plant cannot show: it has **no tyre friction limit** (a linear tyre: a wet or icy road, a braking driver in a curve and a correction of 4 m/s² all behave as on a dry road, so nothing here says whether the car would slide) and **no perception** (the lane-keeping controller sees its lane exactly, so no perception insufficiency, wrong line or late detection is searched; the faults are injected at the actuator side). The driver is a model with stated reactions, the EPS response to each fault is assumed (M-01, M-02), and road users beside the ego are not modelled: the time to the lane line is how soon an adjacent vehicle or barrier would be reached, not whether it is hit.

### EC-N1: an assisted lane change that does not stop at the target lane

HE-02.5 rates a loss of lateral control during an assisted lane change as the car stopping partway and straddling the two lanes. The search finds the opposite effect. A frozen command (F-03) is passed by the panda and keeps the lateral acceleration of the moment it froze, so the car keeps moving sideways through the target lane toward the one beyond it. A loss late in the change (a SoC stall, F-04b, or an EPS fault, F-07) leaves the car with its lateral speed and nothing to stop it at the target lane's centre, so it runs over the far line. Either way the departure is into a lane the driver did not check before the change. No hazardous event of the HARA names that effect.

Rows: EC-11, EC-12, EC-13. Queued for the project lead as DQ-405 in the decision register.
