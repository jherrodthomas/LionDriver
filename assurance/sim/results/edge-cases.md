The worst cases of every search family of [`families.yaml`](../search/families.yaml) (WP-V-04 U-4), each re-run with its parameters rounded as a scene writes them, without its fault (is it the fault or the road and the traffic?), and under assumption probes (which stated assumption decides the outcome). Each row is mapped to the HARA hazardous event (HE-, [WP-C-03](../../02-concept/WP-C-03-hara.md)) or SOTIF hazard (SH-, [WP-C-05](../../02-concept/WP-C-05-sotif-hazard-identification.md)) it falls under. A departure or contact that matches no hazardous event is a finding for the HARA: it is listed under [New-hazard findings](#new-hazard-findings) and queued in the [decision register](../../01-management/decisions/register.yaml); the HARA itself is not edited here.

Lateral families: rho is the margin of the body edge to the lane line (rho < 0: a departure). Longitudinal and hand-over families: rho is the smallest bumper gap to the lead or the follower (rho < 0: contact).

What the plant cannot show: it has **no tyre friction limit** (a linear tyre: a wet or icy road, a braking driver in a curve and a correction of 4 m/s² all behave as on a dry road, so nothing here says whether the car would slide) and **no perception** (the lane-keeping controller sees its lane exactly, so no perception insufficiency, wrong line or late detection is searched; the faults are injected at the actuator side). The driver is a model with stated reactions, the EPS response to each fault is assumed (M-01, M-02), and road users beside the ego are not modelled: the time to the lane line is how soon an adjacent vehicle or barrier would be reached, not whether it is hit.

What the plant cannot show: it has **no tyre friction limit** (the driver's braking of up to 8 m/s² and the lead's are taken as given on any road; on a wet road (W02) about 5 m/s² is what the tyres give, so a row that needs more is worse there) and **no perception** (the ACC sees its lead at once, exactly and at any range, so a lead detected late, a stopped vehicle not taken as a lead (SH-06) or a cut-in is not searched; low visibility acts only through the driver). The ACC is a model, not openpilot's MPC; the lead brakes at a constant rate; the following driver brakes after a reaction time to the ego's brake lights (on above 1 m/s²) at a set deceleration until no faster than the ego, and does not steer round it. The driver is a model with stated cues and reactions; the PCM response is assumed (M-03).

## New-hazard findings

### EC-N1: an assisted lane change that does not stop at the target lane

HE-02.5 rates a loss of lateral control during an assisted lane change as the car stopping partway and straddling the two lanes. The search finds the opposite effect. A frozen command (F-03) is passed by the panda and keeps the lateral acceleration of the moment it froze, so the car keeps moving sideways through the target lane toward the one beyond it. An EPS fault late in the change (F-07) leaves the car with its lateral speed and no assist to stop it at the target lane's centre, so it runs over the far line even with the siren. (A SoC stall late in a change, F-04b, did the same before the siren on revocation, DQ-404; it now stays just inside.) Either way the departure is into a lane the driver did not check before the change. No hazardous event of the HARA names that effect.
