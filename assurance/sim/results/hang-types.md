# Hang types of the safety MCU: reset time and what the car does

> **Synthetic data (DSR-12).** Generated by [`plant/hang_types.py`](../plant/hang_types.py); do not edit by hand. The real panda safety code of the reference build runs in the loop with its IWDG feed (`drivers/hw_watchdog.h`); the five hangs are those of the firmware's fault-injection build (`drivers/fault_injection.h`, WP-V-05 VS-FI-17), placed as `panda_loop.HANGS` says. The IWDG counter is modelled from the registers the firmware writes. EPS, driver and road are the plant's stated assumptions ([plant-results.md](plant-results.md)). **No evidence credit** (DQ-139). Closes SIM-OI-3.

## 1. What each hang stops, and when the IWDG resets

The request comes at 10 phases 13 ms apart, against the 10 ms safety task and the 125 ms tick. Times run from the request, or from the moment the hang starts; the reset is when the IWDG counter reaches zero. Every interrupt has the same priority, so a hang inside one stops them all. LSI 32 kHz (nominal).

The item's frames stop when the hang starts (a hang that stops everything) or at the reset (main loop, early feed). After the reset the switch goes to pass-through, and the stock camera path is back, or it stays in intercept and nothing reaches the car. **What this shows** (generated from the rows above):

- A main-loop hang resets 90–99 ms after it starts, not the timeout alone: the main loop sets its flag just before it spins, so the next safety task still feeds the IWDG once. The hangs that stop everything reset 80–90 ms after they start. The watchdog impact record's D-1 ("detection time is the timeout only") holds for those, not for the main loop.
- During a main-loop hang the panda still runs its safety task and its CAN and host interrupts, so the item's frames keep passing its checks until the reset. That buys little: with the switch going to pass-through and the EPS releasing, the body reaches the lane line at 1.58–1.59 s against 1.51–1.52 s for interrupts off (today's F-05).
- With the LSI swept: the main-loop hang stays within 100 ms down to 32 kHz; the tick hang stays within 100 ms down to 30 kHz; interrupts off stays within 100 ms down to 30 kHz; the safety-task hang stays within 100 ms down to 30 kHz. Below the nominal 32 kHz the main-loop hang has no margin; the datasheet's range (OI-2) decides whether it meets TSR-501.
- A second feed straight after the first resets 0 ms after it at every LSI swept: the window (HWSR-501b) catches a safety task that runs too fast.
- With the switch stuck in intercept nothing reaches the car after the reset in any hang; the drift then runs until the driver corrects, as in F-05's stuck-intercept case.
