# Tunnel entry and exit: how long the road camera is badly exposed

> **Synthetic data (DSR-12). Design aid, no evidence credit (DQ-413).** Generated by [`weather/tunnel.py`](../weather/tunnel.py) with openpilot's auto exposure ported from camerad ([`camera_ae.py`](../weather/camera_ae.py)); do not edit by hand. Luminances are typical orders (CIE 88), not a real tunnel; the sensor's sensitivity is an assumption until local logs give the exposure on a sunny road.

At 110 km/h: the sunlit approach, then the threshold zone at 5 % of it for 150 m, a transition to 10 cd/m² inside, a 1 km tunnel and the exit into daylight. The loop runs at 20 frames a second; a frame is badly exposed when its grey is more than 1 stop from the target. The exposure cannot go below 14.6 (2 lines at the always-on conversion gain of 7.32).

| Case | Entry: badly exposed frames (dark / bright) | Entry: last one after | Exit: badly exposed frames | Exit: last one after | Exposure floor reached |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominal (4000 cd/m² outside, settles at exposure 30) | 5 (5 / 0), 250 ms | 0.70 s | 21 (0 / 21), 1050 ms | 1.47 s | no |

| Less sensitive sensor (settles at exposure 120) | 5 (5 / 0), 250 ms | 0.70 s | 21 (0 / 21), 1050 ms | 1.47 s | no |

## What this shows (generated)

- **Entry:** 5 frames (250 ms) badly exposed, 5 too dark and 0 too bright, the last 0.70 s after the portal. A dark frame tells the loop how far off it is (the grey is not clipped), so it corrects in a few steps; it overshoots briefly because it acts on the same dark frames again while its first correction is still in flight (three frames of latency).
- **Exit:** 21 frames (1050 ms) badly exposed, the last 1.47 s after the exit, up to 25 frames in the cases above: the exit is the longer window. An overexposed frame tells the loop less (its grey is near the top of the gamma curve), so it steps down only a little each frame.
- Inside, the loop settles at an exposure about 217 times the daylight one, well within the sensor's range.
- For KS-12 and TC-18: a validation drive through tunnels must cover windows of about a second at each portal where the road camera's image is far from its target. How the driving model copes with them is the question for the logged drives; this says only how long they last.
