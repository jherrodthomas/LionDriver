## What this shows (generated)

- **The device loses 18 K of cabin air** between a clean fan and a stalled one: the SoC reaches its limit at 50 °C clean and 32 °C stalled.
- **Most of the loss comes late.** Half of it is gone only once 80 % of the cooling is lost; with 40 % left the limit is reached at 44 °C. A fan that still turns keeps most of its value, so a stalled-fan check (HWSR-509a) catches the case that matters most.
- **The host still stops first** at every step: the SoC reaches its limit at a lower cabin air than the MCU's junction would reach its own, so the MCU stays in range while the host runs its thermal policy.
- For WP-O-03 (maintenance): a dusty fan is worth a note in the user information; the device's fan speed and temperatures in its own logs show it before it stalls.
