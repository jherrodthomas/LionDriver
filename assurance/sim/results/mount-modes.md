## What this shows (generated)

- The first mode is at 91 Hz (56–117 Hz over the pad's band): the device nods (pitch) on the pad. It sits 3.4 times above the top of the idle band and 5.7 times above the wheel order; the engine's cruise order overlaps the band of the first mode, so a log at cruise should look for it.
- The road's own shaking, filtered by the suspension, is small up there. The road-driven jitter is at most 0.06 px RMS even at 2 % damping, and most of it is the pad giving a little under the body's slow bounce, below the mode, so the damping hardly matters. Far below a pixel: a sound mount does not blur or shift the road camera's image through the road.
- For KS-16 and calibration drift: the risk is a mount that is not sound (a soft or failing pad, play in the hinge), which this does not model. For WP-H-07 QE-08: the modes give comma's vibration qualification a frequency to look at, not a pass.
