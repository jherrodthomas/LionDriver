The SoC starts an assisted lane change to the left at 6 s, following a cosine path over `lc_s`; the fault starts at phase `phase` of it (0 start, 1 end). The driver's cue is a drift of 0.2 m from the planned path, and the driver completes the change. Robustness is the margin to the outer lines of the two lanes (corridor): the right line of the ego lane and the left line of the target lane.

Faults: F-01b hard-over (right), F-03 frozen command, F-04b SoC stall, F-07 EPS fault. Robustness: rho is the margin of the body edge to the outer lines of the ego lane and the lane to its left after the fault; rho < 0 means it crossed one. Beyond about −2 m the car is in the next lane and the magnitude means little; the sign and the time to the line are what count.

What the plant cannot show: it has **no tyre friction limit** (a linear tyre: a wet or icy road, a braking driver in a curve and a correction of 4 m/s² all behave as on a dry road, so nothing here says whether the car would slide) and **no perception** (the lane-keeping controller sees its lane exactly, so no perception insufficiency, wrong line or late detection is searched; the faults are injected at the actuator side). The driver is a model with stated reactions, the EPS response to each fault is assumed (M-01, M-02), and road users beside the ego are not modelled: the time to the lane line is how soon an adjacent vehicle or barrier would be reached, not whether it is hit.
