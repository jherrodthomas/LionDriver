# A weight on the steering wheel: what it does to driver monitoring and the steering

> **Synthetic data (DSR-12). Design aid, no evidence credit (DQ-413).** Generated by [`weather/wheel_weight.py`](../weather/wheel_weight.py) with the Toyota port and driver monitoring read and pinned from opendbc and openpilot; do not edit by hand. The torque counts have no published scale: the band is the override study's ([`override-l2.md`](override-l2.md)), until M-08 measures it.

`steeringPressed` is set above 100 counts of driver torque; the car controller drops lateral control at 500. With 500 counts taken as 2–5 N·m, the press threshold is 0.4–1.0 N·m. A weight on a 18.5 cm rim:

| Where it hangs | Reads as the driver steering | Steering still holds the lane against it | Lateral control drops |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 or 9 o'clock | 0.22–0.55 kg | up to 0.83–1.65 kg | 1.1–2.8 kg |
| halfway up (45°) | 0.31–0.78 kg | up to 1.17–2.34 kg | 1.6–3.9 kg |

## What this shows (generated)

- **A few hundred grams reads as a driver's hand.** 0.22–0.55 kg at the side of the rim sets `steeringPressed` for as long as it hangs there.
- **With the driver camera blind, that switches driver monitoring off.** As soon as the camera finds no face (covered, dark) monitoring falls back to the wheel-touch policy, unless its alert is already orange; there a steady `steeringPressed` resets awareness on every frame, the TSR-634 distance bound with it, so no alert ever comes. In the vision policy the same weight does nothing unless the driver also looks attentive.
- **The planned fix does not catch it as worded.** FM-05(b) would restore awareness only for torque "above a threshold for a minimum time": a weight is both, all the time. A test for a change in the torque or the wheel's angle, or FM-05's cap on how often wheel-touch may restore awareness, would.
- **The weight also uses steering authority.** At the press threshold it takes 13–67 % of full LKA torque, leaving 0.66–1.73 m/s² of the controller's 1.99 (study 26) for the road. Heavier than 0.83–1.65 kg the car drifts toward it; from 1.1–2.8 kg lateral control drops, which the driver would notice.
- For the DM clinic (WP-C-08 V-5): a weight of about half a kilogram at the side of the rim with the driver camera covered. For FM-05: add a variation test to (b). The torque scale (M-08) turns the bands into numbers.
