## Standing water deeper than the tread (NASA)

Horne's formula gives full dynamic hydroplaning at **98 km/h** at 35.0 psi (91 km/h at 30.0 psi, 103 km/h at 38.0 psi): below the 110 km/h bound. It applies where water stands deeper than the tread: wheel ruts, puddles at low spots, a worn tyre.

## What this shows (generated)

- In light rain (below 2.5 mm/h) the texture drains the water on every road here: no film, no hydroplaning from rain alone.
- The film model brings the hydroplaning speed down to 110 km/h from 24.8 mm/h on the worst road here, 3.3 times the top of moderate rain, so only in rain the ODD excludes.
- Standing water is the case to watch: NASA's 98 km/h is below the ODD bound. Excluding heavier rain does not exclude a puddle or a rut holding water after the rain, which the ODD's rain bound does not address.
