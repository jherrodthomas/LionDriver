## What this shows (generated)

- With a valid roll estimate the crown itself is taken out: the filter follows a crown change with a 0.05–0.45 s time constant (the code's process noise, 1°/√s, against the localizer's roll noise); the error peaks at 0.11 m/s² and leaves at most 17 cm/s of sideways speed, a small, brief disturbance for the lateral control.
- Body roll is taken out by the fitted torque gain; what is left is under 0.02 m/s².
- **The device's mount roll is the largest crown-related term.** Calibration takes it as zero, so a 1° tilt is 0.17 m/s² (34 % of the margin) until torqued learns `latAccelOffset`, which starts from its default after a fresh install or a parameter reset. A level at installation measures it (an INS check and T1).
- **When the roll estimate is invalid** (localizer roll noise above 1.5°, roll out of range, sensors not OK) paramsd observes zero roll and the estimate decays within about 7 s: the whole push returns, 0.24 m/s² on a crown and 0.59 on a 6 % bank, more than the margin. How often roll is invalid on the highway is a question for local logs (`liveParameters` roll and its standard deviation).
- So the 0.5 m/s² margin covers crown while the roll estimate is valid and the mount offset is learned; it does not cover a steep bank with roll invalid, nor a crosswind (DQ-415).
