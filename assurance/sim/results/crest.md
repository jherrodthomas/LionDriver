## What this shows (generated)

- **The camera sees a little farther than the driver** (228 m against 221 m on a 110 km/h crest), being higher. **The radar sees less** (172 m), being lower: over a crest the radar is the last to see a stopped car.
- On a crest built for 110 km/h, the driver stopping fully on a dry road needs 105 m: inside what the camera sees. On a wet road with 3 mm of tread it takes 158 m, still inside. The item's own −3.5 m/s² needs 149 m, inside the radar's sight.
- **Older crests built for 100 km/h** (K 52) cut every sight distance: the camera sees 191 m, the radar 144 m.
