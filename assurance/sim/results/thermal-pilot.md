Design aid, no evidence credit (DQ-413).** Generated by [`thermal/thermal_pilot.py`](../thermal/thermal_pilot.py) from [`params.yaml`](../thermal/params.yaml); do not edit by hand. **Every input is an estimate until step T1** (device measured, power from the device logs), so these numbers show what the model does, not what the device does.

- Worst edge of the band: 43.4 °C (driven by `conductivity x0.7`). A candidate is read from the worst edge, never the nominal line.
- It becomes a number the ODD can use only after a measured hot drive confirms the model (T4, DQ-412); the bound itself is set in WP-C-02 by the lead.
