## What this shows (generated)

- **A lead at the follow gap stays in the road camera's frame** on every ODD curve: at most 14.8° off our heading (relaxed, 40 km/h), inside ±20°. It is inside the model's narrow input (±15.7°) too.
- **A wall or cutting on the inside hides a stopped car well inside the item's stop.** At 110 km/h on the ODD's 374 m curve the camera sees its tail lights from 95 m (narrow shoulder) or 120 m (wide), against 149 m for the item and 188 m for a driver in the wet. Even with the wide shoulder the sight falls short of the item's stop at 100, 110 km/h.
- **A safety-shape barrier hides the lower part of the car, not all of it.** The sight line to the tail lights meets the barrier near the stopped car, where it is already down near their height: at 110 km/h a 0.81 m barrier hides them beyond 100–126 m, a 1.07 m one beyond 95–120 m. The roof stays in view over either at any distance, so the model sees the top of the car only.
- So TC-35's late detection comes from what stands on the inside of the curve, not from the camera's frame. The ODD's curve bound alone does not leave a stopped car in view at the item's stopping distance where a wall, cutting, hedge or glare screen lines the inside. For KS tests in curves: a stopped car beyond a walled or cut inside edge.
