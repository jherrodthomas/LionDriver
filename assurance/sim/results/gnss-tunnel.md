# Losing GPS in a tunnel: what openpilot does with the GPS

> **Synthetic data (DSR-12). Design aid, no evidence credit (DQ-413).** Generated by [`weather/gnss_tunnel.py`](../weather/gnss_tunnel.py) from openpilot's code, every line it rests on pinned; do not edit by hand. The GPS half of TC-18; the lighting half is [`tunnel.md`](tunnel.md).

## What this shows (generated)

- **There is no position to drift.** openpilot's localizer estimates the device's motion from the inertial sensors and the camera, not a position from the GPS; lateral and longitudinal control use that motion. Losing the GPS in a tunnel changes nothing they read.
- **selfdrived notices but does nothing visible:** after 1500 m without a fix (49 s at 110 km/h) it raises noGps, which has no alert, and the GPS service is on its ignore lists, so a lost GPS is never a communication fault.
- **The tunnel's risk is its light, not its GPS:** the road camera's exposure at the portals ([`tunnel.md`](tunnel.md)) is what TC-18 has to validate.
- No reader of the GPS beyond the reviewed ones.
- For WP-C-06 TC-18: the GNSS-loss half needs no validation drive of its own; the pins here fail if openpilot starts using the GPS while engaged.
