> **Synthetic data (DSR-12). Design aid, no evidence credit.** Generated by [`weather/crosswind.py`](../weather/crosswind.py) from the side elevation measured on the Corolla mesh ([`corolla-e210-geometry.json`](../figures/corolla-e210-geometry.json)) and the plant's Corolla; do not edit by hand. **Textbook models** (slender-body side force, crossflow drag) on an artist's mesh: a band, not a measurement. Steady wind square to the road; gusts are not modelled.

The push is steering-equivalent: the lateral acceleration the controller must add to hold a straight line, as the plant's straight-drift study adds it. Margins: the ODD keeps **0.5 m/s²** for controller error, road crown and wind (WP-C-02 §3.2); the controller's limit is **3.0 m/s²** (`drive_helpers.py`). Wheelbase on the mesh 2.71 m against opendbc's 2.67 m; centre of pressure at 0.988 m against the CoG at 0.221 m (x forward): ahead of the CoG, so the wind also turns the nose away from it.

## What this shows (generated)

- The KS-02 wind (30 km/h) pushes the Corolla at 110 km/h with 0.86–1.76 m/s²: beyond the ODD's 0.5 m/s² margin.
- The upper estimate uses the whole margin from 2.3 m/s (8 km/h) of wind at 110 km/h; Beaufort 7 (near gale) starts at 13.9 m/s.
- With assist lost silently, the plant's straight-drift page gives the time to the 3.7 m lane line for these pushes (F-04b, 80–110 km/h): 0.1 m/s²: never; 0.2 m/s²: 2.99–3.12 s; 0.4 m/s²: 1.91–2.03 s; 0.6 m/s²: 1.34–1.44 s.
- Gusts are stronger than the steady wind and arrive in a second; they are the next step (a gust profile into the plant), together with a measurement on a windy day.
