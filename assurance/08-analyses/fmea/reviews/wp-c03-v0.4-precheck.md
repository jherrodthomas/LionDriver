## Defects found in the tool (continuing the list in the HARA, FSC and item-definition pre-checks)

13. **hara-checklist-reviewer** counts table rows in `06_Operating_Environment` as situations, including non-situation rows, and does not multiply out the weather/surface dimension.
14. **hara-checklist-reviewer** treats tab 13's candidate goals as the safety goals and checks their placeholder safe states. It does not read the "Consolidated into" column, so a HARA that consolidates goals is rated on the wrong goal set.
