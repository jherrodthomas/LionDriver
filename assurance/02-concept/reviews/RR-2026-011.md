## Source and its limits

## What a higher exposure would change (model run, 2026-10-19)

The off-highway misuse situations OS-03, OS-04, OS-14, OS-15 and OS-16 were re-rated in `hara_model.py` and the goals re-derived. The run was made without changing the committed model.

| Off-highway exposure | Goal ASILs as rated | If every ⚠ AoU fails |
|---|---|---|
| E2 (today, A-ODD) | SG-01 C, SG-02 C, SG-03 C, SG-04 B, SG-05 B, SG-06 C, SG-07 B, SG-08 A | SG-01 D, SG-03 D, SG-04 C, SG-05 C; the others unchanged |
| **E3** (1–10 % of engaged time off the highway) | **No goal changes.** Off-highway rows reach C (H-02 OS-14/OS-16, H-07 OS-16), equal to the goals | No goal changes |
| **E4** (> 10 %, e.g. a user who drives rural roads every day, or an ODD that includes them) | **SG-02 rises to D**: a silent loss of lateral control in a rural curve or tight curve (H-02 at OS-16 and OS-14; H-07 at OS-16) is S3 E4 C3. The other goals are unchanged | SG-02 D as well |

So:

- Re-rating the off-highway misuse to E3 is supported by the evidence and costs nothing in ASIL.
- Bringing rural roads into the ODD, which makes them E4 for the users who drive them, raises SG-02 to ASIL D. That holds unless the curve bound is enforced on those roads, or the curve case is shown to be controllable (C2).
- The city adds intersections, traffic lights the item does not detect, and VRUs at the lane edge. Those are functions the item does not have (WP-C-03 §7.1), so the city is not an ODD the current item can support.
