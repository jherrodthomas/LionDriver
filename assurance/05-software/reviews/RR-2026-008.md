## 1. Main result

WP-W-02 tracks the reference build up to opendbc `7993399` / panda `8aa8671`. Most other software documents were written at `8b8c6ae` and were not updated when the nine CT-1 changes were integrated on 2026-10-12. They still describe the old behaviour as "today", for example a 1 Hz RX timeout, no IWDG, `0xc5` ungated and `0x344`/`0x411` passing. Only one change record (TX rate) names any of WP-W-01, -05, -09 or -11 as affected; the other eight name none. This is the root cause of most findings (rule P-03 in WP-M-14 §2.3 now addresses it).
