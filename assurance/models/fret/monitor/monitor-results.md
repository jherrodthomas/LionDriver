# Runtime monitors from the FRETish requirements (TE-03 P4)

> Generated by [`run_monitor.py`](run_monitor.py); do not edit by hand. AI-drafted, not reviewed. No evidence credit: the harness is not the vehicle, and the generated monitor is not part of the firmware.

NASA Ogma turned FRET's past-time formulas for FR-307a, FR-516c, FR-516-duration ([`monitor-spec.json`](monitor-spec.json)) into a Copilot program, and Copilot generated a C99 monitor from it. [`harness.c`](harness.c) runs the panda's own `engaged_mismatch.h` and `heartbeat_siren.h` (pinned submodule) in the order of `safety_task_handler()`, one 100 Hz tick at a time, and the monitor watches every tick. A violation is one call of the monitor's handler for that requirement. FRET's past-time formulas hold "historically" (H), so a violated requirement stays violated: the tables give the tick of the first violation.

Revoked and siren after: ticks (10 ms) from the first "not engaged" heartbeat in the scripted scenarios; — means none. Each scenario runs in its own process, because the generated monitor has no reset. The random scenarios start not-engaged episodes of 0.1 to 1.2 s at 2 % of the heartbeats, drop and restore PCM cruise about every 4 s and re-grant authority, so they exercise the revocation and the siren repeatedly (counts above).
