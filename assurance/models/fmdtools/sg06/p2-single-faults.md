# SG-06 single faults (step P2)

> **Synthetic (DSR-12).** Generated by [`sweep.py`](sweep.py) from the fmdtools model ([`model.py`](model.py), [`events.yaml`](events.yaml)); do not edit by hand. No evidence credit: the results point reviewers at possible gaps in the WP-A-04 tree, and a tree changes only after a person reviews the finding.

Each of the 23 failure modes is injected alone, 0.5 s into a run, in each road phase. Times are seconds after the injection; a cue is a delivered SoC alert, the panda siren, the cluster cruise indicator changing on the host's ACC cancel, or the driver feeling the end of ACC deceleration on a supply loss. TE-06: deceleration lost and no cue within 1.0 s.

## Road phases

| Phase | End of ACC deceleration felt | Source |
|---|---|---|
| following | no | Assumed: following a lead at a steady gap, ACC asks for little deceleration, so its end gives the driver little to feel. AOU-12 does not state the situation |
| approach | yes | UE-6.07, MCS-06.9: the driver notices the end of ACC deceleration while the item brakes for a slower lead or a stop. The default phase of a run: the tree's case, a loss when the situation needs deceleration |

The model has no speed or gap, so a phase changes only what the driver can feel on a supply loss.
