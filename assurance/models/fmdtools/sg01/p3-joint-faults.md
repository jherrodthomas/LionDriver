## Limits

- The model has no torque magnitudes: a command is in limit, excessive, or stale. Whether a given torque is controllable is the plant's and the vehicle tests' (AOU-01R).
- Pruning supersets assumes nothing more is learned from a superset of a set already found.
- Sets above order 3 are not run; the tree's effective sets have at most 3 events.
