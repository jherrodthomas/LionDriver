# SG-03 single faults (step P2)

> **Synthetic (DSR-12).** Generated by [`sweep.py`](sweep.py) from the fmdtools model ([`model.py`](model.py), [`events.yaml`](events.yaml)); do not edit by hand. No evidence credit: the results point reviewers at possible gaps in the WP-A-04 tree, and a tree changes only after a person reviews the finding.

Each of the 26 failure modes is injected alone, 0.5 s into a run, in each phase. A cell gives the TE-03 branch the run reaches and when (seconds after the injection): **excess**, acceleration above the bound, or a step to it, executed by the PCM (G3.1, G3.6); **closing**, in-bound acceleration toward a closing lead, not stopped by the driver (G3.2); **unwanted**, acceleration the driver does not want (G3.3); **gas**, acceleration added on top of the driver's pedal (G3.4); **stale**, stale acceleration held beyond the FTTI (G3.5); **overlap**, stock ACC with the item beyond the FTTI (G3.7). The FTTI is 1.0 s; acceleration the driver does not want counts once it has lasted the SG-05 release time, 0.2 s.
