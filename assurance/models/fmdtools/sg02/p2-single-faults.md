# SG-02 single faults (TE-02 step P2)

> **Synthetic (DSR-12).** Generated by [`sweep.py`](sweep.py) from the fmdtools model ([`model.py`](model.py), [`events.yaml`](events.yaml)); do not edit by hand. No evidence credit: the results point reviewers at possible gaps in the WP-A-04 tree, and a tree changes only after a person reviews the finding.

Each of the 25 failure modes is injected alone, 0.5 s into a run, in each road phase. Times are seconds after the injection; a cue is a delivered SoC alert, the panda siren, or the driver feeling the end of LKA torque on a supply loss. TE-02: control lost and no cue within 1.0 s.

## Road phases

| Phase | Curvature asked | End of LKA torque felt | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| straight | no | no | Assumed: on a straight the LKA torque is near zero, so its end gives the driver little to feel. AOU-01R and AOU-12 do not state the road. The plant shows it matters: with a crown or crosswind push of 0.1 m/s² or more the 3.0 m lane line is crossed before the driver corrects ([straight-drift](../../../sim/results/straight-drift.md)) |
| curve_entry | yes | yes | AOU-01R: the EPS ends LKA torque and the driver feels it while the item holds a curve |
| mid_curve | yes | yes | AOU-01R, as curve_entry. The default phase of a run: the tree's case, a loss while the item holds a curve |

The model has no road geometry, so curve entry and mid-curve give the same result. Where along a curve a fault falls is searched in the plant ([search-fault-timing-curve](../../../sim/results/search-fault-timing-curve.md)).
